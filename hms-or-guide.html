1. Introduction

The Operating Room (OR) Module is designed to manage and document the patient's surgical journey, from surgical booking and pre-operative preparation through the operation, post-anesthesia recovery and post-operative management.

The module supports coordination between OPD, IPD, ICU, the Operating Room, PACU and the Finance/Payment Module. It provides structured documentation of surgical activities, patient transfers, clinical handover and financial references.

1.1 Purpose

  • Organize and manage surgical bookings and operating room schedules.
  • Document pre-operative assessments and orders.
  • Record intraoperative surgical and anesthesia information.
  • Maintain complete operation notes.
  • Document PACU recovery and reassessment.
  • Support postoperative care in IPD and ICU.
  • Integrate surgical bookings with the hospital's Finance/Payment Module.
  • Maintain traceable patient transfer and handover records.

1.2 Scope

This guide defines the proposed functional workflow and documentation requirements for the OR Module. It is a system implementation proposal, not a clinical protocol or substitute for hospital-approved clinical policies.

2. Patient Origin and Entry into the OR Workflow

A patient may enter the surgical workflow from any of the following hospital departments:

  • OPD: A patient assessed in the outpatient department and referred for a planned surgical procedure.
  • IPD: An admitted patient requiring a surgical procedure during hospitalization.
  • ICU: A critically ill patient referred to the Operating Room with documented referral and clinical handover.

2.1 Patient Identification

The OR Module should retrieve or identify the patient's existing hospital record rather than creating an unrelated duplicate patient record. Relevant patient and admission references should remain linked to the surgical encounter.

2.2 ICU-to-OR Referral and Handover

When a patient is referred from ICU to the OR, the system should support:

  • ICU referral documentation.
  • Patient identification and admission reference.
  • Referral date and time.
  • Referring department and responsible staff.
  • Clinical handover notes.
  • Receiving OR confirmation.
  • Transfer date, time and receiving staff documentation.
Important: The ICU-to-OR transfer must be documented as a linked patient movement. The patient's ICU admission should not be treated as closed merely because the patient has moved to the OR.

14. OPERATING ROOM (OR) MODULE

14.1 Surgical Booking

The surgical booking screen is used to register and schedule a planned or urgent surgical procedure. It should link the booking to the patient's existing hospital record and the appropriate patient origin.

Required Surgical Booking Fields

  • Preoperative Diagnosis
  • Planned Procedure
  • Surgeon
  • Assistant Surgeon
  • Anesthetist

14.2 Supporting Booking Information

  • Patient identification and admission reference.
  • Patient origin: OPD, IPD or ICU.
  • Scheduled date and time.
  • Assigned operating room.
  • Surgical priority, according to hospital policy.
  • Booking status and relevant documentation.

14.3 Surgical Schedule

The module should provide a surgical schedule showing booked procedures, assigned operating rooms, scheduled times and booking statuses. Changes to a booking should remain traceable.

14.4 Surgery Payment Integration

When a surgical booking is saved, the system should create a linked finance item in:

OR Surgical Booking
→
Payment Module
→
Surgery Payment

The Finance Department manages pricing and payment records. Finance selects the applicable pricing option:

Payment Option Description
Direct Surgery Price Charges for the surgical procedure only.
Offer Package May include surgery service, medication/pharmacy, consumables and other treatments.

The OR Module should display the linked finance reference, selected payment type and finance status. Pricing, billing and payment transactions remain under the Finance/Payment Module.

Emergency surgery: Pending financial clearance must not automatically block emergency surgery. Emergency exceptions and related authorization must follow the hospital's approved policy.

15. PRE-OPERATIVE EVALUATION

The Pre-operative Evaluation section records the assessment and readiness documentation before the patient proceeds to surgery.

15.1 Surgical Assessment

  • Diagnosis
  • Procedure Planned

15.2 Anesthesia Evaluation

  • ASA Classification
  • Airway Assessment
  • Allergies

15.3 Pre-operative Checklist

  • Consent Signed
  • Blood Available
  • Site Marked
  • Investigations Reviewed

15.4 Supporting Operational Checks

Where required by hospital policy, the workflow may also document patient identification, procedure confirmation, fasting status, removal of jewelry and other preparation checks.

The system should record checklist completion and relevant documentation. It should not independently determine clinical readiness or invent clinical clearance rules.

16. PRE-OPERATIVE ORDERS

This section records the pre-operative orders associated with the planned surgical procedure.

  • NPO Status
  • IV Fluids
  • Antibiotic Prophylaxis
  • Blood Products
  • DVT Prophylaxis

Orders should be documented with the relevant order details, responsible staff and date/time information as supported by the hospital's existing clinical workflow.

The system provides documentation fields. Medication selection, dosages, timing and clinical decisions must follow approved hospital orders and protocols.

17. INTRAOPERATIVE RECORD

The Intraoperative Record documents the surgical and anesthesia activities performed during the operation.

17.1 Surgical Record

  • Procedure Performed
  • Operative Findings
  • Estimated Blood Loss
  • Complications

17.2 Anesthesia Record

  • Drugs Administered
  • Monitoring Record
  • Fluid Balance

17.3 Additional Operational Documentation

The system may also support the following operational records:

  • Circulating Nurse Record
  • Instrument and Sponge Counts
  • Count Discrepancy and Action Taken

The record should support date/time documentation and identification of the staff responsible for the recorded information.

18. OPERATION NOTE

The Operation Note is the structured documentation of the completed surgical procedure. It should be linked to the patient and the corresponding surgical encounter.

Required Operation Note Fields

  • Preoperative Diagnosis
  • Postoperative Diagnosis
  • Procedure Performed
  • Findings
  • Specimens Sent
  • Complications
  • Estimated Blood Loss
  • Surgeon
  • Assistant
  • Anesthetist

The operation note should be saved as part of the surgical encounter and remain available for subsequent postoperative care and review.

19. PACU MODULE

The Post-Anesthesia Care Unit (PACU) is the dedicated recovery stage following surgery and anesthesia. It provides a structured place to document the patient's arrival, recovery observations, reassessment, recovery progress and disposition.

19.1 PACU Arrival

The system should document PACU arrival date/time, receiving staff and the handover information received from the Operating Room.

19.2 Recovery Assessment

Required PACU Fields

  • Vital Signs
  • Pain Score
  • Consciousness Level
  • Aldrete Score

19.3 Repeated Observations and Reassessment

The PACU screen should support multiple observation entries rather than a single one-time assessment. Each entry should allow recording of its date/time and the relevant recovery observations.

Reassessment and recovery progress should be documented throughout the patient's PACU stay according to hospital-approved procedures.

19.4 Recovery Documentation

  • PACU arrival and receiving handover.
  • Repeated vital signs and observations.
  • Pain score documentation.
  • Consciousness level documentation.
  • Aldrete score documentation.
  • Reassessment and recovery progress notes.
  • Disposition and transfer documentation.
  • Handover and receiving confirmation.

19.5 PACU Disposition

After the recovery stage, the system should support documentation of the intended receiving destination:

  • IPD
  • ICU

The receiving unit, transfer date/time, sending staff, receiving staff and handover details should be documented.

Important: This guide does not define clinical PACU discharge criteria, Aldrete thresholds or the Aldrete scoring formula. These must follow the hospital's approved clinical policy.

20. POST-OPERATIVE MANAGEMENT

Post-operative management continues after surgery and recovery in the patient's receiving department. Depending on the patient's care pathway, the receiving department may be IPD or ICU.

Required Post-operative Management Fields

  • Analgesics
  • Antibiotics
  • IV Fluids
  • Diet
  • Mobilization
  • Postoperative Progress Notes

20.1 Continuity of Care

Postoperative documentation should remain linked to the patient's hospital admission and surgical encounter. Clinical information from the operation and PACU should be available to support continuity of care in IPD or ICU.

20.2 Ongoing Documentation

The receiving department should document postoperative management and progress according to its existing clinical workflow. The OR Module should retain the relevant surgical record and transfer information.

Important: Closing the OR surgical encounter does not automatically close the patient's entire hospital admission. The patient may continue receiving care in IPD or ICU.

IPD / ICU

3.2 OR-to-PACU-to-IPD

  1. The patient completes the surgical procedure.
  2. The intraoperative record and operation note are documented.
  3. The patient is transferred to PACU.
  4. PACU arrival, observations, reassessments and recovery progress are documented.
  5. Disposition and handover to IPD are recorded.
  6. IPD continues postoperative management.

3.3 OR-to-PACU-to-ICU

  1. The patient completes the surgical procedure.
  2. The intraoperative record and operation note are documented.
  3. The patient is transferred to PACU.
  4. PACU recovery observations and reassessments are documented.
  5. Disposition and handover to ICU are recorded.
  6. ICU continues postoperative management.

3.4 Direct OR-to-ICU Transfer

The system must also support a direct transfer from the Operating Room to ICU when clinically authorized. In this pathway, PACU may be bypassed.

Operating Room
→
Direct ICU Transfer

The direct transfer must include the relevant authorization according to hospital policy, transfer date/time, sending and receiving staff, clinical handover and receiving confirmation.

PACU must not be treated as a mandatory stop for every patient. The workflow should support both PACU-based recovery and the clinically authorized direct OR-to-ICU pathway.

4. Patient Transfer and Handover Management

Every movement between hospital departments should be traceable. The system should record the movement and confirm that the receiving department has accepted the patient.

4.1 Transfer Information

  • Patient identification and admission reference.
  • Sending department and destination department.
  • Transfer date and time.
  • Sending staff.
  • Receiving staff.
  • Handover information.
  • Receiving confirmation.

4.2 Patient Location

The patient's current hospital location should be updated through the documented transfer process. The system should retain the movement history rather than simply replacing the previous location without a trace.

5. Finance and Payment Integration

5.1 Integration Workflow

Surgical Booking Saved
→
Linked Finance Item
→
Surgery Payment
→
Finance Status Displayed in OR

5.2 Finance Responsibilities

  • Select Direct Surgery Price or Offer Package.
  • Manage the applicable pricing information.
  • Manage billing and payment transactions.
  • Maintain the finance reference and payment status.

5.3 OR Responsibilities

  • Create the linked finance item when the surgical booking is saved.
  • Display the linked finance reference.
  • Display the selected payment type and finance status.
  • Maintain the relationship between the booking and finance record.

The OR Module should not independently create or modify finance-owned pricing and payment transactions.

Emergency surgery exceptions and financial authorization must be handled according to hospital-approved policies. The software should not automatically prevent emergency care because a payment status is pending.

6. OR Encounter Closure and Admission Continuity

The OR surgical encounter and the patient's hospital admission are related but separate records.

  • The OR encounter contains the surgical booking and operation documentation.
  • PACU contains the recovery documentation where the patient follows the PACU pathway.
  • IPD or ICU continues the patient's postoperative care.
  • Closing the OR encounter must not automatically close the patient's hospital admission.
  • Transfer and receiving confirmation must remain available as part of the patient's hospital record.

7. General System Requirements

7.1 Patient Record Linking

Surgical records, pre-operative documentation, intraoperative records, operation notes, PACU observations and postoperative records should be linked to the correct patient and relevant hospital admission.

7.2 Date and Time Documentation

Relevant clinical and operational entries should retain their recorded date/time and responsible staff information.

7.3 Record Traceability

The system should maintain a traceable history of important documentation, status changes, transfers and handovers according to hospital information governance requirements.

7.4 Department Coordination

The OR workflow should support coordination with OPD, IPD, ICU, PACU and Finance without replacing the responsibilities of those departments' existing modules.

8. Items Requiring Hospital Confirmation

The following items should be reviewed and confirmed by the hospital before final implementation:

  • Approved roles and permissions for surgical booking, evaluation, documentation and closure.
  • Operating room scheduling and booking approval rules.
  • Hospital-specific consent and surgical checklist requirements.
  • Pre-operative and anesthesia documentation requirements.
  • Clinical policies for PACU recovery and disposition.
  • Hospital-approved criteria for direct OR-to-ICU transfer.
  • Emergency surgery and financial exception procedures.
  • Required signatures, timestamps and audit requirements.
  • Integration details with existing patient, admission, PACU and Finance modules.
  • Hospital-approved clinical terminology and documentation formats.
This document intentionally does not prescribe medication regimens, clinical thresholds, clinical scoring formulas or medical decisions. These must be supplied and approved by the hospital's authorized clinical teams.

Conclusion

The proposed HMS Operating Room Module provides an organized workflow for surgical booking, pre-operative preparation, intraoperative documentation, operation reporting, PACU recovery and postoperative management.

It supports patient movement from OPD, IPD or ICU into the OR and subsequent transfer to PACU, IPD or ICU, including a direct OR-to-ICU pathway when clinically authorized. It also establishes a link between surgical booking and the hospital's Finance/Payment Module while preserving finance ownership of pricing and payment records.
