Surgery Payment Integration

Linked finance records from surgical bookings

The OR module sends the surgery booking reference to Payment Module → Surgery Payment. Finance is responsible for price selection, package configuration, billing and payment records.

Finance Pricing Options

Direct Surgery Price

Charges for the surgery procedure only.

Offer Package

May include surgery service, medication / pharmacy, consumables and other treatments.

Linked Finance Status
Emergency surgery must not automatically be blocked by payment clearance. Emergency exceptions and authorization rules must follow the hospital's approved policy.
